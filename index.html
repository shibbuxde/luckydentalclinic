Treatments

What we can do for your smile

Six treatments cover almost everything our patients walk in with. If you need something else, we will tell you plainly and guide you.

01

Dental Implants

Permanent, natural-feeling replacements for missing teeth — planned digitally, placed carefully, reviewed on your X-ray with you.

02

Root Canal Treatment

Save a painful tooth instead of removing it. Modern rotary treatment, done gently and usually completed in a visit or two.

03

Cleaning & Polishing

Ultrasonic scaling that removes tartar and stains, followed by a polish — the simplest way to keep gums healthy.

04

Crowns, Bridges & Dentures

Strong, natural-looking restorations for broken, weak or missing teeth — matched to your bite and your budget.

05

Braces & Aligners

Straighter teeth for teenagers and adults, with a clear plan of timelines and visits before you start.

06

Kids Dentistry

Patient, friendly first experiences for children — fillings, fluoride and habit guidance without the fear.

Questions

Patients usually ask

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are seen when the chair is free, but a quick call or WhatsApp message guarantees your slot.

Will the treatment hurt?

We work with proper anaesthesia and a gentle hand, and we stop whenever you raise a hand. Most patients are surprised at how easy it felt.

How much will my treatment cost?

You will get a clear estimate after your check-up, before any treatment begins — no mid-treatment surprises.

Do you treat children?

Yes — kids are welcome, and first visits are kept short, friendly and fear-free.
